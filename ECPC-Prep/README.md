## The postmortem format

After every virtual contest, one file, five lines:

1. Problems solved, and the time each was solved at.
2. The first problem not solved — and whether the obstacle was the *idea*, the
   *implementation*, or the *reading*.
3. Wrong submissions, and the cause of each.
4. The one thing that would have gained the most points.
5. What to drill this week because of (4).

Point 2 is the whole exercise. Most lost problems in a team contest are lost to
misreading a statement or to an implementation that ran long, not to a missing
algorithm — and those two causes have completely different cures.
